Privacy policy

Niks Radio for Android · Effective 8 November 2026

Niks Radio is made by Niks Studio. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: no account, no ads, no advertising ID, no location permission, and we never sell data.

What stays on your device

Your favourite stations, recently played stations, listening history (used for “Your Signal”), selected country and theme are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Usage analytics

To understand how the app is used and to improve it, the app sends usage events to Amplitude, which processes them on our behalf.

DataDetails
App activityApp opened, station tuned (station ID, name and country), play, pause, favourite added or removed, shuffle, country changed.
Random install IDA random identifier created by the app on first launch. It is not your advertising ID or a hardware ID. Clearing the app's data creates a new one.
Session IDThe start time of the current listening session, used to group events.
App and device informationApp version, operating system name and version, and platform (Android).
Approximate locationAmplitude receives your IP address when events are sent and may use it to estimate a coarse location (country or city). The app does not use your device's location.

Analytics data is not shared with anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not sold. It is kept for as long as it is useful for improving the app, within Amplitude's retention limits.

Radio stations and the station catalogue

Station lists come from the community Radio Browser service, and audio streams come directly from each station's own server. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and the requests your device makes. When you tune a station, the app tells Radio Browser that the station was played, which helps rank popular stations. Their own privacy practices apply.

Cast

If you cast to a Chromecast device, Google Play services and the Cast device handle the connection under Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

Internet access, to load stations and play audio. Notifications (Android 13 and later), for playback controls. Foreground service and wake lock, to keep audio playing in the background.

Children

Niks Radio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset the install ID and delete on-device data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. To ask about or request deletion of analytics data, contact us using the developer email on the app's Google Play page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
